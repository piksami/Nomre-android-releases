# Privacy Policy for Nomre

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

Nomre is a spaced-review study tracker. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens with your data.

## The short version

Nomre doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no sign-up, and nothing about your lessons, subjects, progress, photos or voice notes is ever sent anywhere. Everything lives only on your own device.

## What's stored, and where

Everything you enter in Nomre — subjects, lessons, review dates, progress, your name if you give one, and your settings — is saved **only on your device** (locally, in the app's own data folder on Windows, or in the app's private storage on Android). Nothing is uploaded to a server, a cloud account, or to me. I never see it.

## Files, photos and voice notes

You can attach files, photos and voice notes to a lesson. They are copied into the app's own storage on your device and stay there. They are never uploaded anywhere.

- **Photos** are shrunk when you attach them, and any location data inside the photo is removed.
- **Voice notes** are recorded only while you are looking at a recording on screen that you started yourself, and you can pause, stop, save or discard at any time. On Android, a recording keeps going if you turn the screen off or switch to another app, and the phone shows a notification for as long as it runs. Nomre never records in the background on its own.
- **Deleting a lesson keeps its files.** To remove a file, remove it from the lesson with its ✕ button. Uninstalling the app on Android deletes everything, including attached files.

## Permissions (Android)

Besides internet access (used only to check for updates), the Android app can ask for:

- **Microphone**, only when you tap Record, to record a voice note.
- **Camera**, only when you tap Camera, to take a photo for a lesson.
- **Notifications and a foreground service**, only while a voice note is recording, so Android lets it keep recording with the screen off and so you can see that it is running.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. Nomre keeps working; you just can't use that feature. On Windows, the app may ask to use the microphone the first time you record, and uses it only for that.

## Exporting and importing lessons

Nomre lets you export your lessons to a file and import them later, or on another device. This only happens when you choose to do it: you pick where the file is saved (or opened from) using your device's own file picker. The file goes exactly where you put it — Nomre doesn't send it anywhere on your behalf.

When you export, "Include my files and photos" decides whether your attached files travel inside the same file. It is ticked by default, so look at it before you send the file to someone: anything attached to the lessons you export, including voice notes, will be in it.

## Checking for updates

Nomre occasionally checks GitHub to see if a newer version is available (this is the only automatic network request the app makes). This just asks "what's the latest release?" — no personal data, lesson content, or identifying information is included in that request. As with any web request, your IP address is visible to GitHub as part of normal internet infrastructure, but Nomre doesn't attach anything else to it.

## If something goes wrong (crash details)

If Nomre hits an unexpected error, it saves a small technical note **on your device only** — the error message, a stack trace, which screen you were on, and basic device/browser info. It never includes your lesson content, subject names, or anything personal. This stays local and is never sent automatically. If you want to report a bug, you can tap "Copy crash details" in Settings and paste it into an email yourself — that's the only way it ever leaves your device.

## Links you might tap

A couple of buttons in Nomre open something outside the app, only if you tap them:

- **"Buy me a coffee"** opens [coffeete.ir](https://www.coffeete.ir/piksami) (an Iranian donation platform) in your browser. What happens on that site is covered by its own privacy policy, not this one.
- **"Send an email"** opens your email app with a message addressed to me. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

## No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Nomre has no analytics SDKs, no ad networks, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

## Age

Since Nomre doesn't collect any personal data from anyone, it's fine for users of any age, including students under 13 or 16.

## Changes to this policy

If anything about how Nomre handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
